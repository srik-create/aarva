Add Aarva to your Home Screen

{# iOS Safari instructions #}

Open Aarva like a regular app — no install, no permissions.

  1. 1 Tap the Share button (square with an up arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. 2 Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3 Tap Add in the top right.
{# Android instructions — only shown if beforeinstallprompt didn't fire #}

Open Aarva like a regular app — no install, no permissions.

  1. 1 Tap the menu button (three dots) at the top right of Chrome.
  2. 2 Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
{# Desktop / other browsers — generic guidance #}

Look for an Add to Home Screen or Install option in your browser's share or menu icon.

On desktop Chrome or Edge, the address bar shows an install icon when a site is installable.
