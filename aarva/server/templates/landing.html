What Aarva does

Every day, Aarva selects a small set of long-form journalism pieces — essays, reporting, ideas — across a span of topics. We narrate them and shape them into a single daily edition you can listen to like a podcast.

You also get a Crosscut each day: two articles played back-to-back with a bridge between them, surfacing connections you'd otherwise miss.

Pieces are chosen for rigour and editorial integrity, not for engagement. No breaking news. No anxiety cycle. Journalism that delights, educates and expands the mind.
