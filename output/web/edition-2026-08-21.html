What she finds is that when you put a group of models in a shared environment, their problems stop being purely technical. Instead of simply completing tasks, the agents invent bureaucratic busywork and talk each other into false memories. A field report from the AI Village, an ongoing experiment where 27 different artificial intelligence agents communicate and work together on shared goals.
