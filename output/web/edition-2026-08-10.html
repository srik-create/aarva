But as Kimmig explains, the sudden appearance of heavy, nutrient-dense waste sinking to the ocean floor opened up entirely new habitats. The resulting chat offers a surprisingly clear look at how the most ignored byproduct of life actually built the modern ecosystem.

An interview on the evolutionary impact of prehistoric animal waste, based on a recent study of Cambrian fossils. Tracing the record from microscopic droppings to larger pellets, the discussion outlines how the development of animal guts changed early oceans.
