Almost anyone who watches a symphony orchestra eventually wonders what the person waving the stick is actually doing. The job looks like an elaborate pantomime, and an orchestra of highly trained professionals could technically play the notes without any help. But the piece pauses on the invisible mechanics of a hundred musicians trying to breathe and move as a single body. It turns out the real work on the podium has very little to do with keeping time, and everything to do with organizing human attention.

An examination of orchestral conducting, tracing the profession to the industrial expansion of 19th-century orchestras. An account of how thousands of Uyghur militants joined the Syrian civil war to fight against the Assad regime.
