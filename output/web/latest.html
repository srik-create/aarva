Why Global Trepanation?

A 21 October 2026 essay from Astral Codex Ten takes on this archaeological mystery. Why did ancient people across unconnected continents all decide to perform brain surgery with stone tools? The explanation involves high rates of prehistoric violence and a medical misunderstanding that lasted for thousands of years.

A historical and medical explanation for the widespread prehistoric practice of trepanation, or skull-drilling. Early surgeons successfully treated severe head trauma without antibiotics, leading to occasional dramatic recoveries that turned the procedure into a generalized cure-all.
