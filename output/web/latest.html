The piece holds up because it uses Perel's lens to diagnose something bigger than a bedroom slump. It asks what happens to a society when it decides to trade all its curiosity for security.

An examination of the American decline in sexual intimacy, using psychotherapist Esther Perel's theories on desire to explain a broader retreat from pleasure.
